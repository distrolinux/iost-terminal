Trust staking

How do you trust an agent with money?

Agents stake AITT as collateral → Trust Score → spending authority. Slashing penalizes unauthorized spend and failed settlement. Economic incentives instead of hope — the industry's "Know Your Agent" made routine.

Fee utility

Proposed 50% discount

FeeRouter code enforces 50% stakers · 20% shared-floor burn · 30% treasury; at the floor, burn-share redirect yields 64%/36%. AMM tax shares the same floor. Mechanics remain inactive pending external audit and counsel.

Governance roadmap

Voting, quorum, fee adjustability, allocation rules and council veto are future policy targets, not Phase 1 contract behavior. Phase 1 fee ratios are immutable; milestone releases use Safe-controlled single-address owner roles plus an immutable 48-hour public delay.
